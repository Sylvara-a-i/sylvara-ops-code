# U.S. GAAP Topic Reference

## Status And Authority

- Topic map reviewed: **2026-08-04**.
- Sylvara applicability conclusions: **Unknown** until facts, reporting basis, current Codification, effective dates, and qualified review establish them.

The [FASB Standards page](https://fasb.org/standards) identifies the Accounting Standards Codification as the authoritative nongovernmental U.S. GAAP source. Accounting Standards Updates explain amendments but are not the authoritative source after codification. Use authorized [Codification access](https://fasb.org/page/PageContent?isStaticPage=true&pageId=%2Fstaticpages%2Fcodification-access.html) to verify exact paragraphs, definitions, scope, effective dates, transition, disclosures, and private-company alternatives.

This repository stores topic locators and original control summaries only. It does not reproduce Codification text or assert that U.S. GAAP is Sylvara's required reporting basis.

## Current Update Watch

At the review date, two recent updates are especially relevant to a services and software business:

- [ASU 2025-06](https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202025-06.pdf) makes targeted changes to internal-use software guidance. The published update states an effective date for annual periods beginning after December 15, 2027 and permits early adoption. Do not adopt early or change capitalization logic without verifying current Codification, transition requirements, reporting period, and an approved decision.
- [ASU 2025-05](https://fasb.org/page/PageContent?pageId=%2Fprojects%2Frecently-completed-projects%2Fcredit-losses-topic-606-receivables.html) addresses credit-loss accounting for current receivables and contract assets arising from Topic 606 transactions. Verify effective dates, elections, transition, and private-company options before use.

An update watch is a research trigger. It is not an automated policy change.

## Sylvara Decision Questions

Before approving a conclusion, document:

- whether U.S. GAAP is required, elected, contractually requested, or used only as a reference;
- the legal entity, reporting period, financial-statement users, and private-company status;
- the exact contract, transaction, asset, obligation, customer promise, and cash-flow facts;
- applicable scope exceptions, definitions, recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure, effective date, and transition;
- alternatives, estimates, materiality, private-company elections, and consistency with prior periods;
- federal and state tax treatment separately from book treatment; and
- the approved ledger, subledger, disclosure, control, and review implementation.

## Professional Review Gate

Qualified accounting review is required before asserting U.S. GAAP compliance or adopting material conclusions for revenue, contract costs, software or cloud implementation, credit losses, income taxes, leases, contingencies, equity compensation, acquisitions, consolidation, fair value, foreign currency, related parties, subsequent events, going concern, or changes and error corrections.

Zoho configuration, an account label, invoice status, payment receipt, cash movement, customer-facing contract language, or historical treatment cannot decide these questions by itself.
