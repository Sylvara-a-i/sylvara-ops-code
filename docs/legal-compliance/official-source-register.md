# Official Legal And Control Source Register

**Verified:** 2026-08-04

**Status:** Dated primary-source index; not a legal opinion or citator report

This register contains the controlling text and official materials used for the current synthesis. A source's presence does not mean it applies to every workflow. Statutes and regulations are **binding only when their jurisdiction, role, conduct, definitions, thresholds, and effective dates are met**. Orders and enacted acts require scope/effective-date analysis. Agency explanations are official guidance, not substitutes for controlling text. NIST and PCI materials are voluntary or contractual standards, not statutes.

The machine-readable duplicate, artifact provenance, and verification date are in [`reference/source-manifest.json`](reference/source-manifest.json). Recheck the live source, amendments, effective date, cases, and local law before relying on any entry.

## Interpretation And Update Rules

- Current eCFR and code text outrank an older summary.
- A proposed rule is not a final rule. `FED-FCC-AI-NPRM` is included to track the inbound distinction and pending AI disclosure proposal, not to claim that the proposed disclosure is codified.
- The 2026 FCC waiver is narrow; do not describe all revocation duties as delayed.
- The vacated FCC one-to-one-consent revision never became effective; do not archive it as current law.
- CAN-SPAM is an email authority, not an SMS authority.
- “One-party state” does not resolve interstate choice of law, every covered technology, later use/disclosure, employee monitoring, or civil claims.
- The representative recording table is not a fifty-state matrix.
- State privacy thresholds and exemptions change. A client can impose processor duties even when Sylvara is below a direct controller threshold.
- Re-verify on every change trigger and before counsel or an operator approves a jurisdiction, campaign, client, recording flow, regulated workflow, or provider role.
