# Copywriting Editing And QA Playbook

## Purpose

Review strategy, truth, clarity, originality, and operational readiness in separate passes. Editing everything at once hides material problems behind sentence polish.

## Pass 1: Strategy

- Is there one primary audience, buying situation, outcome, and action?
- Does the asset perform the correct job for its channel and funnel stage?
- Is the selected angle relevant, differentiated, supportable, and compatible with the offer?
- Does each section change understanding, belief, or readiness?
- Is anything included only because a template or swipe file contained it?

If strategy fails, return to the brief before line editing.

## Pass 2: Truth And Evidence

- Map every material claim to the evidence ledger.
- Verify capabilities, integrations, pricing, terms, dates, availability, support, and security statements.
- Confirm consent and approved wording for proof, testimonials, logos, and cases.
- Remove invented numbers, certainty, credentials, outcomes, urgency, and scarcity.
- Preserve limitations and conditions that would change a reasonable decision.

## Pass 3: Clarity

- Can the intended reader understand the value and next step on the first read?
- Replace category jargon and internal language with accurate customer language.
- Break long sentences when they contain more than one decision.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over abstract business language.
- Put qualifiers beside the claim they limit.
- Remove ambiguous pronouns, unexplained acronyms, and vague references.

## Pass 4: Specificity

Increase specificity through verified audience, situation, workflow, deliverable, condition, mechanism, time frame, or evidence. Do not add decorative numbers.

Replace:

- broad audience labels with a relevant situation or role;
- generic improvement with the exact operational effect;
- vague method with the real process;
- empty superlatives with proof or a fair comparison; and
- unclear next steps with the exact action and expectation.

## Pass 5: Structure And Pace

- Lead with the most relevant question or value.
- Keep one main idea per paragraph or section.
- Put proof close to claims.
- Resolve objections before they block the next action.
- Use subheads that communicate meaning, not labels such as Overview or Benefits.
- Remove repeated points that add no new evidence or clarity.
- Make the offer, terms, and call to action easy to find.

## Pass 6: Voice

Compare the draft with the approved voice behaviors:

- directness;
- formality;
- energy;
- technical depth;
- confidence;
- warmth;
- preferred vocabulary; and
- prohibited tendencies.

Do not use a named writer or third-party brand as the voice specification. Remove inflated language that makes Sylvara sound less credible than the evidence.

## Pass 7: Originality

If private inspiration was used:

- compare openings, headlines, section order, metaphors, labels, examples, rhythm, and calls to action;
- search for distinctive phrases or unusually similar sequences;
- rewrite from the approved brief without looking at the source;
- preserve only the abstract decision or structure; and
- document the result as original synthesis.

Name replacement, close paraphrase, and source-shaped sentence sequences fail this pass.

## Pass 8: Friction And Accessibility

- Headings and links describe their destination.
- Calls to action use clear action language.
- Forms request only necessary information.
- Error, confirmation, and next-step messages are understandable.
- Mobile line length, paragraph length, and button labels are usable.
- Meaning does not depend only on color, image, audio, or layout.
- Images, captions, and media have appropriate accessible equivalents.
- Reading level fits the audience without oversimplifying material terms.

## Pass 9: Compliance And Privacy

- No private data, confidential facts, production identifiers, or hidden metadata.
- No unsupported financial, health, legal, employment, immigration, safety, or regulated claim.
- No fabricated testimonial, review, endorsement, result, deadline, inventory limit, or affiliation.
- Commercial intent and sponsorship are clear where required.
- Consent, unsubscribe, preference, platform, and targeting requirements are addressed.
- Price, billing, renewal, cancellation, guarantee, and eligibility terms are visible and accurate.

## Pass 10: Final Readback

Review the exact final surface, not only the source document:

- page or message renders correctly;
- links and forms reach the intended target;
- offer state and price are current;
- personalization fields do not leak or fail awkwardly;
- tracking does not include private values;
- approved copy matches the published copy; and
- rollback or correction ownership is clear.

## Scoring Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience relevance | Generic or unclear | Partly specific | Immediate and specific |
| Message clarity | Confusing | Understandable with effort | Clear on first read |
| Offer clarity | Missing or vague | Mostly defined | Scope, terms, and next step are explicit |
| Differentiation | Commodity language | Some distinction | Meaningful, supportable reason to choose |
| Proof | Missing or mismatched | Partial | Strong and claim-matched |
| Objection handling | Ignored | Some concerns addressed | Material decision risks resolved honestly |
| Voice | Off-brand | Mixed | Consistent and owned |
| Originality | Source-shaped | Some generic overlap | Clearly original synthesis |
| Compliance | Material issue | Review needed | Cleared for proposed use |
| Privacy | Private or unapproved data | Minimization or review incomplete | Cleared and minimized for proposed use |
| Action | Competing or unclear | Present | Obvious and proportionate |

Offer clarity, proof, originality, compliance, privacy, and action are blocking dimensions. Each must score `2` before publication; a score of `0` or `1` blocks. A high total score does not offset an incomplete blocking dimension.

## Variant Review

Meaningful variants change one strategic variable:

- audience situation;
- desired outcome;
- problem framing;
- mechanism;
- proof lead;
- objection;
- offer framing; or
- call-to-action commitment.

Record what each variant tests. Do not present five cosmetic rewrites as strategic options.

## Handoff Checklist

- [ ] Brief and evidence ledger are current.
- [ ] Strategic angle and primary action are named.
- [ ] Claims, proof, terms, and permissions are verified.
- [ ] Copy passed all ten editing passes.
- [ ] Every blocking rubric dimension scores 2; any 0 or 1 remains unpublished.
- [ ] Unknowns and assumptions are visible.
- [ ] Required approvals are recorded.
- [ ] Exact final surface was read back.
- [ ] Correction or rollback owner is known.

## Validation

The final reviewer should be able to explain why the copy exists, what evidence supports it, what it deliberately avoids, how it is original, what action it requests, and who approved publication.
