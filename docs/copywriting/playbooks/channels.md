# Copywriting Channels Playbook

## Purpose

Adapt message strategy to the reader's context, available attention, funnel stage, platform constraints, and level of commitment. A useful message can fail when placed in the wrong channel or asked to do too much.

## Channel Selection

For every asset, define:

- where the reader encounters it;
- what they were doing immediately before;
- what they already know;
- how much attention is realistic;
- the next appropriate action;
- device and format constraints;
- delivery, consent, accessibility, and platform rules; and
- how success will be measured.

## Website And Homepage

### Job

Orient several qualified audience paths without becoming a generic company brochure.

### Recommended sequence

1. clear category, audience, and primary value;
2. primary action and lower-commitment alternative;
3. recognizable use cases or problems;
4. method or capability overview;
5. proof and trust;
6. offer paths or product areas;
7. objections, implementation, or support; and
8. final action.

### Quality gate

- A new visitor can explain what the company does, for whom, and why it matters.
- The homepage does not claim every possible audience or outcome.
- Navigation labels match buyer tasks, not internal organization charts.
- Planned capabilities are not presented as live.

## Landing Page

### Job

Move one audience from one traffic context toward one primary action.

### Recommended sequence

- message match with the source ad, email, or search intent;
- specific outcome and audience;
- mechanism or reason to believe;
- proof;
- offer and terms;
- objection handling;
- low-friction action; and
- confirmation of what happens next.

### Quality gate

- One campaign angle and one primary action.
- Headline and offer match the incoming message.
- Form length matches the value and funnel stage.
- Mobile readers receive the same essential truth and terms.

## SaaS Product, Feature, And Pricing Pages

### Product page

Explain the primary job, workflow, differentiated method, capabilities, proof, fit, onboarding, and next step.

### Feature page

Connect one capability to a specific workflow and consequence. Include prerequisites, limits, and related capabilities where material.

### Pricing page

Make plan differences, billing period, usage limits, included support, overages, renewal, cancellation, and buying path clear. Do not hide material conditions behind a call.

### Comparison page

Compare verifiable criteria fairly. State the comparison date and acknowledge situations where another option may fit better.

## Email

### Every email needs

- one reason for being sent now;
- one primary reader takeaway;
- one primary action;
- an honest subject line and preview;
- a clear sender identity;
- consent and preference compliance; and
- continuity with the landing destination.

### Common email jobs

| Email job | Structure |
|---|---|
| Welcome | Confirm expectation, orient, deliver promised value, set the next step |
| Education | Relevant problem, useful insight, application, optional next step |
| Offer | Context, outcome, mechanism, proof, offer, terms, action |
| Objection | Name one reasonable concern, answer it with facts, invite the next step |
| Case evidence | Situation, approach, verified result, limitations, relevance, action |
| Onboarding | Current step, exact action, support, completion signal, next step |
| Reactivation | Acknowledge time, provide fresh relevance, offer preference or exit |
| Deadline | Verified deadline, consequence, terms, action, no manufactured countdown |

### Subject line gate

The subject line may create curiosity but must not misrepresent sender, content, urgency, account status, reply status, relationship, or financial consequence.

### Sequence design

Each message should advance understanding or readiness. Do not repeat the same pitch with different punctuation. Map the sequence across orientation, problem, insight, mechanism, proof, objection, offer, and decision.

## Paid Social Ads

### Job

Earn enough attention from a defined audience to justify the next click, view, or lead action.

### Structure

- audience or situation cue;
- one problem, outcome, insight, or proof angle;
- clear relevance to the visual;
- one next action; and
- landing-page message match.

### Quality gate

- The hook remains truthful without the visual.
- The visual remains understandable without exaggerated body copy.
- No prohibited personal-attribute inference, fake interface, fake notification, or fabricated result.
- Variants test different strategic angles, not trivial wording.

## Search Ads

### Job

Match explicit intent and make the most relevant next step obvious.

### Structure

- reflect the searcher's task or category;
- state meaningful differentiation;
- include verified qualification such as audience, location, integration, or use case when relevant;
- align the call to action with intent; and
- send the reader to the closest matching page.

Do not use competitor names or claims without approved legal and platform review.

## Organic Social

### Useful post types

- practical lesson;
- point of view;
- process breakdown;
- myth correction;
- approved case insight;
- checklist;
- product explanation;
- behind-the-scenes operating principle; or
- invitation tied to a real offer.

Lead with relevance, deliver standalone value, and make the action proportionate. Do not turn every post into a disguised hard sell.

## Advertorial And Educational Lead

### Job

Help the reader understand a problem, category, or mechanism before presenting an offer.

### Structure

- transparent labeling and context;
- recognizable situation;
- useful insight or investigation;
- mechanism or category explanation;
- balanced evidence and limitations;
- transition to the offer; and
- clear commercial disclosure and action.

An advertorial must not impersonate independent journalism, hide sponsorship, fabricate reporting, or use invented experts and sources.

## Webinar And Video Sales Script

### Structure

- opening relevance and expectation;
- problem and stakes;
- teaching framework or mechanism;
- demonstration or evidence;
- application to the audience;
- offer and fit;
- objections and terms;
- action; and
- concise recap.

Spoken copy requires shorter sentences, audible transitions, repetition of essential terms, and visuals that support rather than duplicate narration. Do not stretch a weak idea to meet an arbitrary runtime.

## Long-Form Sales Page Or Letter

Use long form when the audience needs education, proof, objection resolution, or offer detail. Maintain a clear through-line from lead to mechanism to proof to offer.

Useful devices include section summaries, descriptive subheads, proof near claims, visible terms, repeated calls to action at natural decision points, and a concise final recap. Open loops must be resolved; do not withhold basic terms merely to force continued reading.

## Checkout, Upsell, And Downsell

### Checkout

- restate the selected product, quantity, price, billing period, material terms, and next step;
- minimize unrelated fields and distractions;
- explain errors and recovery clearly; and
- never preselect paid additions without clear consent.

### Upsell

Offer a complementary next step that improves the original outcome. State incremental price, terms, and effect on the original order.

### Downsell

Offer a genuinely different scope, payment arrangement, or entry point. Do not use a confusing downgrade or hidden recurring commitment.

## Print And Direct Mail

Account for physical context, scanning behavior, response mechanism, production constraints, and accessibility. Make sender, purpose, offer, terms, response path, and deadline visible. Historical print conventions are inspiration, not proof of current effectiveness or compliance.

## Cross-Channel Continuity

Verify that:

- audience and promise remain consistent;
- claim strength does not increase downstream without new evidence;
- pricing and terms match;
- design and copy describe the same action;
- tracking parameters do not expose private data;
- confirmation messages reflect what actually happened; and
- the follow-up sequence honors consent and expectations.

## Validation

- The asset has one defined channel job and primary action.
- Message depth matches realistic attention and decision complexity.
- The next destination delivers on the source message.
- Consent, accessibility, platform, privacy, and disclosure requirements are addressed.
- Terms and claims remain consistent across the funnel.
