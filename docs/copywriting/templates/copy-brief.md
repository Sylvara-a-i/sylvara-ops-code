## Evidence Ledger

| Proposed claim | Evidence source | Evidence owner | Status | Allowed wording | Expiration or review date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | Verified / Qualified / Illustrative / Unknown / Prohibited | | |

Rules:

- `Verified`: supported by an authoritative, current source and usable within its scope.
- `Qualified`: usable only with an explicit limitation or attribution.
- `Illustrative`: clearly synthetic or hypothetical; internal mockups only, never customer-ready copy.
- `Unknown`: research or approval is incomplete; exclude from publishable copy.
- `Prohibited`: legally, contractually, ethically, or operationally unavailable.

Testimonials, customer logos, performance figures, comparisons, awards, credentials, and named integrations require documented permission and substantiation.
