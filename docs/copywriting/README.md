# Sylvara Copywriting Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This directory is the public, reusable copywriting reference for Sylvara. It distills a bounded representative review of a large private swipe collection into original operating guidance that ChatGPT, Codex, and human writers can use without publishing the underlying third-party files.

The source collection is inspiration and research evidence only. It is not a phrase bank, factual source, approved claim library, brand-voice authority, or license to reproduce another writer's work.

## Status

- Repository classification: **Original synthesis from private reference material**
- Raw source files published: **No**
- Source republication rights: **Mixed or unverified**
- Semantic source review: **Representative, not comprehensive; this is not a complete semantic index**
- Customer and production facts: **Not included**
- Approval to publish customer-facing copy: **Required separately**

## Start Here

1. Read the [Originality And Claims Standard](originality-and-claims-standard.md).
2. For Sylvara-specific work, read the current [Managed Receptionist Product Direction](../product/README.md) and preserve every `Proposed`, `Unknown`, and publication boundary it states.
3. Complete the [Copy Brief Template](templates/copy-brief.md) with verified product, audience, offer, proof, voice, and channel facts in an approved private workspace.
4. Choose the relevant playbooks and patterns from the task router below.
5. Draft in original language from the approved brief; never imitate a source's distinctive phrasing.
6. Run the [Editing And QA](playbooks/editing-and-qa.md) review before presenting or publishing copy.

If critical facts are missing, stop drafting and list the exact Unknowns. Polished prose must not conceal an incomplete offer, unsupported claim, or unclear audience.

**Public-repository boundary:** Completed briefs belong in an approved private workspace and must never be committed to this public repository. Keep only the blank template or a fully synthetic, sanitized example in GitHub.

## Agent Workflow

When ChatGPT or Codex writes copy for Sylvara:

1. **Establish the job.** Name the channel, audience, funnel stage, business outcome, and single primary action.
2. **Load facts.** Separate approved facts and evidence from assumptions, ideas, and Unknowns.
3. **Choose one message.** Define the audience, desired outcome, problem, differentiated mechanism, proof, and offer.
4. **Select structures.** Choose only the patterns that fit the audience's awareness and the channel's constraints.
5. **Draft multiple angles.** Vary the strategic angle, not just adjectives or punctuation.
6. **Interrogate claims.** Remove or qualify anything without evidence. Never invent urgency, scarcity, testimonials, results, awards, or customer details.
7. **Make it sound owned.** Use the approved brand voice and product vocabulary. Do not imitate a named writer or source.
8. **Edit in passes.** Strategy, clarity, specificity, proof, friction, voice, originality, compliance, and formatting each receive a separate review.
9. **Present decisions.** Explain the chosen angle, the intended reader action, material assumptions, and any approval still required.

## Source Collection Profile

The private collection contained 1,249 files totaling approximately 2.29 GB. It was dominated by long-form promotions, investing promotions, business and coaching copy, SaaS pages, historical advertising, email, and paid-social examples. Most files were third-party PDFs or images; some were Word documents and videos.

The semantic review used filename and folder analysis plus text extraction from 56 representative files. It supports this bounded synthesis but is not a complete semantic index of all 1,249 files.

Those binaries remain outside GitHub because:

- repository policy blocks public binary archives and oversized files;
- possession does not establish republication rights;
- screenshots, documents, and exports may contain metadata or private information;
- financial, health, relationship, and scarcity claims require evidence and context; and
- a curated operating system is more useful to an agent than an unsearchable binary dump.

See the [source manifest](reference/source-manifest.json) for aggregate counts and the exact publication boundary.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Use source material to study decisions and structure, never to borrow distinctive wording.
- Prefer a clear, believable promise over maximum hype.
- Make the offer and next action obvious.
- Convert features into customer consequences without inventing results.
- Match proof strength to claim strength.
- Treat financial, health, legal, employment, immigration, tax, and safety claims as high risk.
- Never create fake testimonials, customer stories, endorsements, data, reviews, guarantees, deadlines, inventory limits, or credentials.
- Never include customer PII, confidential research, account data, private prompts, or production identifiers.
- Repository approval does not authorize customer-facing publication, an ad launch, an email send, or a website deployment.

## What This Library Is Not

This library is not a substitute for product truth, customer research, brand approval, legal review, platform advertising rules, or performance testing. It provides durable thinking tools. The approved brief and current evidence determine what Sylvara may actually say.
